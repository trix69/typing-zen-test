# Key sounds

Recordings from kbsim by Thomas Lai, https://github.com/tplai/kbsim (commit ba103f3b0afa9dab80447aa2e7e2ed80b6bd80e4),
`src/assets/audio/`, used under the MIT licence in `LICENSE-kbsim.md`. Folders renamed for Typing Zen:

| Typing Zen | kbsim folder | Switch |
|---|---|---|
| `red` | `redink` | Gateron Red Ink (Linear Red) |
| `black` | `mxblack` | Cherry MX Black (Linear Black) |
| `yellow` | `cream` | NK Cream (Linear Yellow; kbsim has no yellow switch) |
| `brown` | `mxbrown` | Cherry MX Brown (Tactile Brown) |
| `blue` | `mxblue` | Cherry MX Blue (Clicky Blue; no SPACE/ENTER/BACKSPACE files) |

Each has `press/GENERIC_R0..R4.mp3` (one per keyboard row) and `release/GENERIC.mp3`, and most have
`press/` and `release/` `SPACE`, `ENTER` and `BACKSPACE`.
